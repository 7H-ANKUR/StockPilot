============================================================
2. CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
============================================================

The product is an intelligence layer over existing retail/POS systems.

Do NOT build a POS replacement.

The architecture must follow:

Retail Data
    ↓
Data Ingestion
    ↓
Validation / Cleaning
    ↓
Canonical Database
    ↓
Feature Engineering
    ↓
ML / Forecasting
    ↓
Inventory Decision Engine
    ↓
LLM Agent
    ↓
Explainable Recommendation
    ↓
Human Approval
    ↓
Purchase Order

The most important architectural rule is:

NUMBERS MUST COME FROM DATA, ML MODELS, SQL, OR DETERMINISTIC BUSINESS LOGIC.

The LLM must not invent:

- sales
- inventory
- forecast numbers
- supplier lead time
- MOQ
- prices
- GST values
- reorder quantity

The LLM is responsible for:

- reasoning
- planning
- tool selection
- tool orchestration
- summarization
- explanations
- natural-language interaction
- workflow coordination

Do not use the LLM as a replacement for SQL, Python calculations, or forecasting models.
